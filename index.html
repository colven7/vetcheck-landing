FAQ

Common Questions

Straight answers to the things people ask before signing up.

Do I have to be a veteran to use VetCheck? +

No. VetCheck is built for veterans, active-duty military, and every public-service profession, including law enforcement, fire service, EMS, federal agencies, and corrections. Anyone who serves, or who has served, is welcome.

Is Atlas a financial advisor? +

No. Atlas is an informational AI assistant only. It helps you understand and explore your tracked data and explains financial concepts in plain language. Atlas does not provide financial, legal, tax, or investment advice. For advice, consult a licensed professional.

Is my data private and secure? +

Your data stays yours. VetCheck applies multiple layers of account protection, encrypted storage, and strict access controls aligned with modern security standards. You can also toggle privacy blur on sensitive numbers at any time. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing.

Does VetCheck connect to my bank? +

No. VetCheck is a manual tracking and informational platform. You enter income, bills, and debts on your terms. We never ask for your bank login or routing information. This keeps you in full control of what gets tracked.

What devices can I use VetCheck on? +

VetCheck runs on iOS, Android, and any modern web browser. Sign in once and your data syncs across every device tied to your account.

What languages does VetCheck support? +

VetCheck is available in 9 languages, with motivational fortunes and core interface fully localized. We continue to expand language coverage to serve members across the globe.

Can I cancel at any time? +

Yes. The Standard plan is month-to-month or annual and you can cancel anytime, no questions asked. Annual subscriptions stop renewing at the end of the paid term. Founding Members keep their no-charge Standard access while their account remains active.

Do I have to write a journal entry every day? +

No. Daily Check-Ins use quick-tap chips — twelve pre-written wins and twelve pre-written blockers that rotate by day of the week, so Monday's prompts are different from Friday's. Tap whatever fits and you're done in under a minute. A free-form box is available if you want to add detail, but it's optional.
