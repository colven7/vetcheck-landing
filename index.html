Platform Features

Everything You Need to
Take Financial Command

Purpose-built for veterans, active-duty service members, first responders, and public service professionals.

Finance Hub
Track every income source, bill, and debt. BAH auto-populates by zip code. Auto-pay toggles on debts. PDF financial reports on demand.
Daily Check-Ins
Quick habit-tracking check-ins with pre-built chip options. Build financial streaks. Streaks reset if you miss a day, keeping discipline real.
Intel Tab
Global reconnaissance and opportunity arsenal, combined into one intelligence hub. Browse 100+ career opportunities and volunteer roles tailored to your sector.
Branch, Rank & Veteran Profiles
Set your branch, rank, call sign, and sector, whether active-duty, reserve, retired, or separated. Built to reflect how you actually served.
Milestones & Goals
Set financial goals and career milestones. Track savings targets, emergency funds, and debt payoffs with visual progress rings.
Security & Privacy
2FA via Authenticator app. 15-minute idle timeout with 1-minute countdown. New-device verification. Your data stays yours.
